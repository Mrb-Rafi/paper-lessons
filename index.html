PLPaper Lessons
Interactive web books

Research papers,
taught from zero.

Each lesson takes one paper and rebuilds it from the ground up: short chapters, a widget for every idea, every equation unpacked symbol by symbol, and quizzes to check yourself. No background assumed. Pick one and start reading.

No lesson matches that search.

How every lesson works

From zeroStarts with the background a newcomer needs, then builds up to the paper’s own methods.
InteractiveSmall models and simulations run in your browser, so you can poke each idea yourself.
FaithfulNumbers quoted from the paper are marked as such and linked to it; toy examples are labelled as toys.
Private progressChapters you mark as read are remembered in your own browser only.
Independent educational companions to the papers they cover — not official publications of the papers’ authors. Each lesson credits its sources and licences.
