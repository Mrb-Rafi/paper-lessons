Modern AI,
in 49 papers.

From word vectors to reasoning models: 49 landmark papers, put in order from easiest to hardest. Each paper gets its own interactive web book that starts from zero, so you can begin anywhere. Following the order is easiest, because each lesson builds on the ones before it.

How this collection works

Easy to advancedLessons are ordered by what they build on, not by year. ★ needs nothing; ★★★★★ is heavy maths or systems.
Every book from zeroEach book opens with the background it needs. Earlier lessons go deeper but are never required.
FaithfulNumbers quoted from a paper are marked and linked; toy examples are labelled as toys.
GrowingLessons appear as they are finished. “Coming soon” cards link to the paper in the meantime.
Independent educational companions to the papers they cover, not official publications of the papers’ authors. Each book credits its sources and licences. ← All lessons
